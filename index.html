01 · About

Who I Am

I'm a Full-Stack & GenAI Engineer based in Maharashtra with a B.E. in Computer Science from Nagpur University. For the past 2+ years I've been shipping production-grade web apps — lately, systems that retrieve, cite, and reason over a user's own sources.

My featured project is LearnForge, a NotebookLM-style study notebook: ingest PDFs, links, and YouTube captions, index them in Qdrant, then stream grounded answers with citations plus quizzes, flashcards, and mind maps. I also built FitAI, an AI fitness coach with streaming LLM responses. I care about retrieval quality, clean architecture, and shipping.

Beyond code, I'm an anime enthusiast and sci-fi buff — usually reading about distributed systems or watching something from the Ufotable catalog.

LearnForge — Turn sources into understanding
